# User Validation Interview Guide

## Purpose
Validate FeedSales AI value proposition with feed sales professionals in North America. Understand workflow pain points, pricing sensitivity, and channel preferences.

---

## Interview Flow (10 Questions)

### Question 1: Introduction & Ice Breaker

**Main question:** "Thanks for taking 15 minutes to chat. Before we dive in, tell me a bit about yourself—what's your role, and how long have you been in feed sales?"

**Follow-up prompts:**
- What region do you cover?
- Do you work independently or with a team?
- What's the most rewarding part of your job?

**Why this matters:** Establish rapport, understand context (experience level, territory, company size).

---

### Question 2: Current Cost Calculation Workflow

**Main question:** "Walk me through how you calculate feed formulation costs today. From the moment a customer asks for a quote to when you send it."

**Follow-up prompts:**
- What's the first step you take?
- Where do you get ingredient prices?
- How do you build the formulation—do you use templates or start fresh each time?

**Why this matters:** Map the exact workflow to identify friction points and integration opportunities.

---

### Question 3: Current Tools Used

**Main question:** "What tools do you use for these calculations? Excel, custom software, pen and paper, something else?"

**Follow-up prompts:**
- Did you build the spreadsheet yourself or inherit it?
- Have you tried any specialized feed formulation software before?
- What works well with your current tool? What doesn't?

**Why this matters:** Identify tool adoption history, willingness to switch, and current tool satisfaction.

---

### Question 4: Pain Points in the Workflow

**Main question:** "What's the most frustrating part of the cost calculation process? The thing that makes you groan every time."

**Follow-up prompts:**
- Is it the time it takes, the accuracy, or something else?
- What happens when prices change mid-process?
- Have you ever sent a quote with an error? What happened?

**Why this matters:** Surface emotional pain points—the "why" behind switching behavior.

---

### Question 5: Time Investment

**Main question:** "How much time do you spend on quoting and cost calculations per day? Or per quote?"

**Follow-up prompts:**
- Is that time consistent, or does it spike during certain periods?
- What could you do with that time if it were freed up?
- Does your team/company track this time? Is it visible to management?

**Why this matters:** Quantify the opportunity cost; anchor pricing conversation.

---

### Question 6: Interest in Faster Solution

**Main question:** "Imagine a tool that could give you a complete cost breakdown in 10 seconds. You describe the diet, it calculates everything—prices, nutrition, compliance. Would that be useful to you?"

**Follow-up prompts:**
- Would you trust an AI-generated calculation, or would you double-check it?
- How fast is "fast enough"—10 seconds, 1 minute, 5 minutes?
- What would make you skeptical about such a tool?

**Why this matters:** Test core value proposition; surface trust barriers and speed expectations.

---

### Question 7: Pricing Sensitivity

**Main question:** "If this tool saved you 2+ hours per week, what would you pay for it monthly? Think about what that time is worth to you."

**Follow-up prompts:**
- Is $29/month too high, too low, or about right?
- Would you prefer a per-quote pricing model instead of subscription?
- Who makes purchasing decisions—you, or someone else in your company?

**Why this matters:** Validate pricing model; identify decision-maker dynamics.

---

### Question 8: Animal Species Focus

**Main question:** "Which animal species do you work with most often—swine, poultry, cattle, or others?"

**Follow-up prompts:**
- Do you specialize in one, or cover multiple?
- Are the formulation requirements different enough to need separate tools?
- What's the most complex species to formulate for?

**Why this matters:** Prioritize species coverage; understand complexity variance.

---

### Question 9: Communication Channel Preference

**Main question:** "Do you use Telegram for work communication? If not, what messaging app or channel do you prefer—WhatsApp, email, phone calls?"

**Follow-up prompts:**
- What makes a channel convenient for you during work hours?
- Do you use messaging apps while on-site (barns, farms)?
- Would you be open to trying Telegram if a useful tool was there?

**Why this matters:** Validate Telegram as entry channel; identify fallback options.

---

### Question 10: Open Sharing

**Main question:** "Anything else you want to share about feed sales, quoting challenges, or what you wish existed to make your job easier?"

**Follow-up prompts:**
- What's one tool you wish someone would build?
- Any pet peeves about how customers request quotes?
- If you could change one thing about your workflow, what would it be?

**Why this matters:** Capture unexpected insights; open space for user-driven innovation.

---

## Interview Best Practices

- **Duration:** 15-20 minutes. Don't overrun.
- **Tone:** Casual, peer-to-peer. Not salesy. Listen more than talk.
- **Recording:** Ask permission. If no, take written notes.
- **Follow-up:** Thank them. Offer early access if they showed interest.

## Post-Interview Log

After each interview, log:
- Key pain points surfaced
- Pricing reaction (positive/negative/neutral)
- Channel preference (Telegram yes/no)
- Species coverage
- Interest level (high/medium/low)
- Contact info for beta invite

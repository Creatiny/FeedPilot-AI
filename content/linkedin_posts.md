## Post 4: NRC standards explained in 60 seconds (W2 - Educational)

NRC requirements. Everyone talks about them. Few actually understand them.

Here's the 60-second version:

NRC = National Research Council nutrient requirements for swine/poultry/cattle.

They're the baseline. Not the target.

Your formulation should:
- Meet NRC minimums (health)
- Exceed where needed (performance)
- Stay within budget (profit)

The problem? NRC tables are dense. Cross-referencing them with ingredient costs takes hours.

That's why we built automatic NRC compliance checks into FeedSales AI.

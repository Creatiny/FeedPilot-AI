# FeedSales AI Beta Signup Form - Field Definitions
# For Google Forms setup
# Created: 2026-03-30

## Form Title
FeedSales AI Beta Program Signup

## Form Description
Join our beta program and get 30 days of free access to FeedSales AI — the AI-powered feed sales assistant designed for North American feed industry professionals.

---

## Field 1: Full Name
- Type: Short text
- Required: Yes
- Question: Full Name
- Validation: None

## Field 2: Email
- Type: Short text
- Required: Yes
- Question: Email Address
- Validation: Email format
- Description: We'll send your beta access details here

## Field 3: Company
- Type: Short text
- Required: No
- Question: Company / Organization Name
- Validation: None

## Field 4: Job Title
- Type: Short text
- Required: No
- Question: Job Title
- Validation: None
- Description: e.g., Feed Sales Representative, Feed Mill Manager, Nutritionist

## Field 5: Livestock Operation Size
- Type: Multiple choice (single select)
- Required: Yes
- Question: How many head of livestock does your operation manage?
- Options:
  - Less than 1,000
  - 1,000 - 10,000
  - More than 10,000
- Validation: None

## Field 6: Species Worked With
- Type: Checkbox (multi-select)
- Required: Yes
- Question: What species do you work with? (Select all that apply)
- Options:
  - Swine
  - Beef cattle
  - Dairy cattle
  - Poultry
  - Other (with text field for specification)
- Validation: None

## Field 7: Current Cost Calculation Method
- Type: Multiple choice (single select)
- Required: Yes
- Question: How do you currently calculate feed costs?
- Options:
  - Excel spreadsheets
  - Specialized software (e.g., Feed formulation software)
  - Manual calculations
  - Other (with text field for specification)
- Validation: None

## Field 8: Biggest Frustration
- Type: Long text (paragraph)
- Required: Yes
- Question: What's your biggest frustration with feed cost management?
- Description: Tell us about the challenges you face — this helps us improve FeedSales AI for your needs
- Validation: None
- Max length: 1000 characters (recommended)

---

## Form Settings
- Confirmation message: "Thank you for signing up! We'll review your application and send beta access details within 2-3 business days."
- Collect email addresses: Yes
- Limit to 1 response per person: Yes
- Edit after submit: No
- See summary charts: Yes
